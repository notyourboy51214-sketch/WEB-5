The Obsidian Room

Absolute Exclusivity.

For those requiring utmost discretion and a bespoke culinary narrative, The Obsidian Room offers a completely isolated dining environment within the restaurant. Concealed behind a mirrored wine wall, the room features its own dedicated entrance, sound-dampening acoustic architecture, and a private sommelier.

The Booking Process

1. The Inquiry

Contact our events director detailing your preferred date, party size, and the nature of the event (e.g., corporate closing dinner, private celebration).

2. The Consultation

You will consult directly with Executive Chef Laurent Dubois and our Head Sommelier to tailor the 12-course tasting menu and wine pairings specifically to your guests' preferences and absolute dietary requirements.

3. The Confirmation

A formal itinerary is drafted. A 50% deposit secures the Obsidian Room, along with dedicated valet coordination for your VIP guests.
