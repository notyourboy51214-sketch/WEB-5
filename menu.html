Chapter VII

Autumnal Narrative.

Our current 12-course menu explores the transition of seasons—the final vibrant yields of summer giving way to the earthy, smoke-driven preservation techniques of autumn. Every ingredient has been sourced based on peak maturity from our global network of artisanal purveyors.

AED 1,200 per guest (excluding pairings)

I. L'Aube (The Dawn)
Hokkaido Scallop Crudo Japan / Brittany

Diver-caught scallops cured in kombu, served with a delicate white peach aguachile, finger lime vesicles, and finished with a drop of cold-pressed Moroccan argan oil.

Pairing: 2018 Domaine Leflaive Puligny-Montrachet
Smoked Oscietra Caviar Caspian / Normandy

Gently smoked over applewood embers, resting on a bed of whipped bone marrow butter and a fermented buckwheat blini.

II. La Terre (The Earth)
Wild Mushroom Consommé Piedmont / UAE

A clarified, intensely earthy broth of foraged porcini and black trumpet mushrooms, poured tableside over a single cured quail egg yolk and local desert truffles.

Pairing: 15-Year Oloroso Sherry, Bodegas Tradición
A4 Kagoshima Wagyu Kyushu / Burgundy

Dry-aged in-house for 45 days. Seared over binchotan charcoal and glazed with a reduction of black garlic and 1998 vintage port, accompanied by a purée of salt-baked celeriac.

Pairing: 2010 Château Margaux, Premier Grand Cru Classé
III. Le Crépuscule (The Twilight)
Gorgonzola Dolce & Pear Lombardy / Rhone

A transition course. Aerated gorgonzola dolce piped into a crisp tuile, balanced with a gel of saffron-poached William pears and candied walnuts.

Valrhona Grand Cru Textures Madagascar / Tahiti

A study in chocolate: 70% Guanaja mousse, smoked sea salt caramel core, tempered cacao nibs, and a quenelle of Tahitian vanilla bean glace.

Pairing: 2005 Château d'Yquem, Sauternes
