OUR CRAFT

Cooking without gas or electricity. Only embers.

Every dish at ATELIER NOIR touches wood fire. We burn aged Danish birch, cherrywood, and binchotan to sculpt temperatures from a searing 800°C hearth down to a gentle 45°C cold-smoke chamber.

Zero Synthetic Oils

Rendered grass-fed beef tallow and cold-pressed pine nut oils only.

Coastal Foraging

Beach mustard, sea buckthorn, and scurvy grass harvested daily at low tide.
